Review of HQCJ recommendations on dismissal for incompetence

Review of decisions and submissions of the High Qualification Commission of Judges of Ukraine on the dismissal of judges based on qualification assessment results due to unsuitability for office.

Submissions where I was rapporteur

The HCJ left all three HQCJ submissions allocated to me without consideration: by the time they were heard, the grounds for dismissal on assessment results no longer existed.

Where the data comes from

  • Decisions on the merits come from the public register of HCJ acts at hcj.gov.ua; the rapporteur and the allocation date come from the HCJ internal case register. Every link has been checked to concern that particular judge.
  • The review period is the number of calendar days from automatic allocation of the submission to the rapporteur until the HCJ decision on the merits. It is not calculated for submissions still pending.
  • Halahan O.V. appears twice: the first dismissal decision was quashed by the Administrative Cassation Court, the matter was heard again, and the court had meanwhile been renamed.
  • Data as of 14 August 2026, the date my term of office ended.
