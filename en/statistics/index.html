Method and sources

HCJ indicators

  • There are no figures for 2022: until 12 January 2023 the High Council of Justice lacked a quorum and considered no cases.
  • The 2026 figures cover January–July only and therefore cannot be compared directly with full years.
  • “HCJ acts” is the total number of acts; procedural rulings are not counted twice, as they are part of the rulings.
  • The criminal-procedure block sums consents to detention and custody together with initial temporary suspensions; decisions extending a suspension are not included.
  • For 2026, decisions on protective measures comprise 34 on judges’ reports and 4 on other materials.
  • Dismissals on HQCJ recommendations in 2026 concern 2 judges under the transitional constitutional procedure.

Disciplinary chamber indicators

  • In 2023–2024 the unit of account was disciplinary complaints; in 2025–2026 it was the conclusions of disciplinary inspectors. “Received / auto-allocated” therefore reflects the total procedural workload rather than the number of unique complaints.
  • The indicator “left without consideration (individually)” exists only in the 2023–2024 reporting forms.
  • The 2025 results also include disciplinary cases opened before the disciplinary inspectors started work.
  • The pending figure is given as of 31 July 2026; annual balances are not summed.
  • HCJ members are assigned to the chambers in which they actually sat during the period.

Source: official summary statistical reports on the work of the High Council of Justice, its disciplinary chambers and its secretariat, together with the consolidated results of HCJ members’ work in the disciplinary chambers.
